# KFARM Agro Limited

A website for KFARM Agro Limited, a fish business in Abia State, Nigeria that supplies fresh and dried fish and teaches practical fishery.

## About the client

KFARM Agro Limited sells catfish and tilapia, fresh and dried, to households, restaurants, retailers, and other bulk buyers. Alongside the fish, KFARM offers fishery training for people who want to learn how fish farming works, from beginners to existing farmers.

## What the client needed

- A clear first impression of what KFARM is and what it offers
- A simple way for customers to order, using the channel they already use: WhatsApp
- A place to present fresh and dried catfish and tilapia
- A wholesale offer for restaurants, retailers, and caterers, including a way for people to bring buyers to KFARM for commission
- A page for the fishery training, including a 50% training discount for customers who have ordered fish
- The business's location, so customers know where to find it

## What was built

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Introduces KFARM, what it offers, and the approach behind it |
| **Fish** | Fresh and dried catfish and tilapia, wholesale supply, how ordering works, and pickup location |
| **Learn Fishery** | The training curriculum, who it is for, and the customer discount |
| **Contact** | WhatsApp as the main way to reach KFARM, plus the map and quick links |

## Design approach

- **Look:** white backgrounds, a deep green ink colour, and a lime accent, set in Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Layout:** photo-led sections with simple card layouts, kept consistent across all four pages
- **Ordering:** every order and enquiry button opens WhatsApp with a message already filled in for that product or enquiry, so customers never have to explain from scratch
- **Honest content:** no invented statistics, and customer testimonials are included only once real feedback is available

React and Tailwind CSS
